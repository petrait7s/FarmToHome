# 04 — Customer Journeys

Five journeys cover the business model. Each lists stages, the customer's goal, the design response and the emotional target.

## Journey A — First visit to founding household (acquisition, pilot phase)

| Stage | Customer goal | Design response | Feeling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arrive (web, often via referral or social) | "What is this, and can I trust it?" | Editorial hero, brand promise, the 5-step journey told visually | Intrigue |
| Explore | "Is the quality real?" | Producer stories, a sample traceability journey and sample test reports, with no sign-up required to browse | Confidence |
| Check availability | "Do you deliver to me?" | Area check early and lightweight (enter area, see slots). If outside the pilot: an elegant waitlist | Respected, not rejected |
| Join | "Make it easy" | Phone/OTP sign-up, then a short **Household Profile** (household size, cooking habits, preferences) in 3 tappable steps, all skippable | Welcomed |
| First market | "Help me start" | **Starter Market** suggestion built from the household profile. Edit it or start blank | Guided |
| First delivery | "Did it arrive well?" | Delivery-day tracking. Unboxing includes QR packs. Post-delivery "Scan your first pack" invitation | Delight |

## Journey B — Monthly planner (core repeat behaviour, fast mode)

1. **Reminder**, 3–4 days before the planned date: "Your Monthly Market is ready to review."
2. **Open** to My Market › Monthly. Last month's basket is pre-filled. Smart notes: "You usually run out of mustard oil early. Add 1 L?" These suggestions are explained and never auto-added.
3. **Adjust** with steppers. Out-of-stock or seasonal items are marked with a suggested alternative from the same category or origin.
4. **Confirm slot**, with the recurring schedule pre-selected.
5. **Done** in under 60 seconds. This is the target metric for fast mode.

## Journey C — Connoisseur discovery (slow mode)

Home › "This Week's Arrivals" › story of a shutki producer in a coastal region › producer profile › product › traceability journey › **Add to Market** or **Add to Monthly plan**. Discovery should be open-ended reading; the buying affordances stay present but quiet.

## Journey D — Scan to story (offline-to-online)

| Stage | Design response |
|---|---|
| Customer holds a pack at home | QR printed with the prompt "Scan to meet your producer" |
| Scans with the phone camera | App installed: deep link straight to the Batch Journey. Not installed: a fast web Batch Journey page with an app invitation (no install wall) |
| Reads the journey | The 5 steps with real dates, producer, grade and reports |
| Acts | Reorder this exact product · follow the producer · share the story |

This journey is a key **trust loop and acquisition channel**: guests and family members who scan a pack become new visitors.

## Journey E — Delivery day and support

1. **Morning:** "Your market arrives today, 9–11 AM". The message includes the delivery partner's name when assigned.
2. **Live status:** packed, then out for delivery, then arriving.
3. **Handover:** contactless or staff-received option. For households where staff receive deliveries, the account holder gets a confirmation.
4. **After delivery:** a quick per-item quality check ("Everything perfect?"). An issue goes **directly to the concierge** with a photo, and the resolution is offered in-app (replace or credit). No forms maze.

## Cross-journey principles

- **Remember the household.** Preferences, quantities, staff instructions and substitution rules are stored once and used everywhere.
- **Never surprise.** Recurring orders always send a review notification before charging or packing. Skip and pause are one tap.
- **Human within reach.** A concierge entry point is visible in checkout, on product pages (for questions) and on delivery tracking.
- **Bilingual at every step.** Language can be switched anytime without losing state.
