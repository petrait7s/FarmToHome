## The design idea in brief

**"A food journal you can shop from."** Editorial luxury minimalism: Warm Ivory space, Sky Blue typography and immersive sections, Harvest Gold detailing, and Red Orange as a sparing signature. The proposal cover's overlapping circles become the brand's graphic and motion motif.

Three ideas carry the product:

1. **My Market:** weekly and monthly household baskets that you review and confirm in under a minute. Optional, never locked in.
2. **The Journey:** every pack's five-step farm-to-home story with certificate-grade data, opened by scanning its QR code.
3. **People and places first:** producers and origin regions are browsable, storied, first-class content.
